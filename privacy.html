Ⅱ unorder
Effective August 10, 2026

Privacy policy

Unorder is designed to help you change a habit without turning your behavior into an advertising profile.

Information we process

Account information

If you use bank sync, Sign in with Apple provides a stable account identifier and, only when you choose to share them, your name or private relay email. Core app blocking works without an Unorder account.

Financial information

If you voluntarily connect an account through Plaid, our service receives a read-only access token, institution name, and transaction details such as merchant, amount, date, and pending status. We use this information to identify food-delivery spending and calculate insights. Unorder never receives your online-banking password or full card number.

Information that stays on your device

Your chosen Screen Time apps, categories, and websites are stored in an Apple App Group on your device. Your budget, danger window, streak, avoided-order count, and notification preference are also stored locally. Apple’s frameworks provide opaque selection tokens; Unorder does not upload your Screen Time selections.

Purchases and notifications

Apple processes subscriptions. The app reads verified entitlement status but does not receive your payment-card details. If you opt in, iOS schedules a local danger-window reminder; Unorder does not use notification permission for advertising.

How we use information

  • Provide read-only transaction sync, delivery classification, spending trends, and protection rules.
  • Authenticate your account, secure connections, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or use cross-app tracking.

Service providers

We use Apple for authentication, subscriptions, notifications, and Screen Time controls; Plaid for optional financial connectivity; and infrastructure providers to operate the Unorder API. These providers process information under their own terms and our service arrangements.

Retention and deletion

Connected transaction data is retained while your connection or account remains active. Disconnecting an institution removes its imported transactions and asks Plaid to invalidate its access token. “Delete account and imported data” removes your server-side account, sessions, connections, and imported transactions. Local settings remain on your device until you reset or remove the app. Limited security or legal records may be retained only when required by law.

Your choices

You can use core blocking without bank sync, decline notification access, omit websites from protection, disconnect each institution, or delete your account in the app. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by email.

Children and changes

Unorder is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes and will post a new effective date here.

Contact

Email support@unorder.app for privacy questions.
