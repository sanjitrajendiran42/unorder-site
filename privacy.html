Privacy policy

Unorder helps you change a habit without collecting your personal data.

Data collection

The current App Store version of Unorder does not create accounts, connect financial institutions, serve advertising, use third-party analytics, or transmit your app activity to Unorder servers.

Information that stays on your device

Your chosen Screen Time apps, categories, and websites are stored by Apple as opaque selection tokens in an App Group on your device. Your monthly guardrail, danger window, friction setting, streak, avoided-order count, sample spending data, and notification preference also remain on your device.

Apple services

Unorder uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity, and local notifications. Apple may process information under Apple’s own privacy policy as part of providing iOS and App Store services. Unorder does not receive your Screen Time selections.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it only to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Unorder does not retain app data on its servers. Local settings remain on your device until you remove the app or reset its data through iOS.

Children and changes

Unorder is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes and will post a new effective date before enabling any feature that changes these practices.
