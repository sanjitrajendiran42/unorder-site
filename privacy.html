Privacy policy

Unorder collects only the information needed to provide features you choose and to improve the product with your permission. We do not sell personal data or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Screen Time information stays on your device

Your chosen Screen Time apps, categories, and websites are represented by Apple as opaque selection tokens. Unorder stores those tokens in its App Group so its Screen Time extensions can shield your selections. Unorder does not upload the selections, tokens, or device-activity reports to its servers or include them in analytics.

Account and connected spending

You do not need an account for core Screen Time protection. If you choose connected spending, Unorder uses Sign in with Apple to create an account. We retain Apple’s account identifier and an optional given name to authenticate and support that account. Apple’s identity credential may contain an email address, but Unorder does not store it.

With your permission, Plaid connects Unorder to a financial institution. Unorder receives a read-only connection token, institution name, and transaction information used to identify and display likely food-delivery purchases. We do not receive your bank password or full payment-card number. Transactions can be pending, delayed, renamed, or corrected; Unorder does not see prices or cart contents inside delivery apps.

Your analytics choice

Product analytics is off until you opt in. Paid functionality does not depend on analytics, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops future analytics events.

If enabled, we receive events such as onboarding completion, protection setup, intervention outcome, paywall source and variant, normalized monthly or annual plan selection, purchase outcome, and restore outcome. Events can also include app/build version, iOS version, language, broad device class, and days since installation.

Each event has a new random identifier used only to prevent duplicate storage. We do not attach a persistent account, device, or advertising identifier. Analytics excludes selected apps/sites, Screen Time tokens or activity, merchants, transactions, institutions, amounts, saved-money values, account identifiers, names, email addresses, precise location, and anything you type.

Information stored on your device

Protection preferences, streak, avoided-order events, amounts you choose to record as saved, savings goal, notification preference, analytics choice, and cached connected transactions are stored on your device. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

Subscriptions and Apple services

Apple processes App Store subscription payments; Unorder does not receive your payment-card number. Unorder also uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity, local notifications, Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy when providing those services.

Retention, deletion, and access

Account and connected transaction data remain in Unorder’s active service while your account or institution connection exists. Disconnecting an institution removes that connection and its imported transactions from Unorder’s active database. The in-app “Delete account and imported data” control removes the Unorder account, sessions, linked institutions, and imported transactions from the active database and clears corresponding cached data after the request succeeds.

Opt-in event-level analytics is deleted after no more than 400 days. Because those events do not contain an account or persistent device identifier, Unorder cannot identify a person’s prior analytics events for individual export or deletion. Connected delivery transactions are visible in the app and can be exported as CSV text from Settings. You may contact us about an applicable access or deletion request.

Plaid and Apple may retain information under their own policies. Operational service-provider logs and backups, where used, are protected and expire under their configured retention schedules.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords, bank credentials, or payment-card numbers.

Children and changes

Unorder is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes and will post a new effective date before materially different practices take effect.
