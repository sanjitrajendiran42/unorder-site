Ⅱ unorder
Effective August 13, 2026

Privacy policy

Unorder collects only the information needed to provide features you choose and to improve the product with your permission. We do not sell personal data or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Screen Time information stays on your device

Your chosen Screen Time apps, categories, and websites are represented by Apple as opaque selection tokens. Unorder stores those tokens in its App Group so its Screen Time extensions can shield your selections. Unorder does not upload the selections, tokens, or device-activity reports to its servers or include them in analytics.

Account and connected spending

You do not need an account for core Screen Time protection. If you choose connected spending, Unorder uses Sign in with Apple to create an account. We retain Apple’s account identifier and an optional given name to authenticate and support that account. Apple’s identity credential may contain an email address, but Unorder does not store it.

With your permission, Plaid connects Unorder to a financial institution. Unorder’s service retains the encrypted read-only connection token, institution name, and connection status needed to request updates. During a sync, the service relays transaction changes to your iPhone and does not retain transaction rows, merchant names, amounts, transaction sync cursors, classifications, or correction rules. Classification and corrections happen locally on your iPhone. We do not receive your bank password or full payment-card number. Transactions can be pending, delayed, renamed, or corrected; Unorder does not see prices or cart contents inside delivery apps.

Private usage insights

Optional coarse usage counts are off until you opt in. If enabled, counts such as onboarding completion or completed pauses are stored only on your iPhone. Unorder does not upload events to a custom analytics endpoint. Paid functionality does not depend on this choice, and turning it off stops new local counts.

Information stored on your device

Protection preferences, streak, avoided-order events, amounts you choose to record as saved, savings goal, notification preference, private-usage-insight choice and counts, connected transactions, per-connection sync cursors, classifications, and correction rules are stored on your device. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior. If you reinstall and sync again, Unorder can rebuild only the transaction history then available from Plaid; previous local corrections do not return.

Subscriptions and Apple services

Apple processes App Store subscription payments; Unorder does not receive your payment-card number. Unorder also uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity, local notifications, Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy when providing those services.

Retention, deletion, and access

Disconnecting an institution removes its connection credential from Unorder’s service and clears that institution’s local transactions and cursor after the request succeeds. The in-app “Delete account and imported data” control removes the Unorder account, sessions, and linked-institution credentials from the service and clears all corresponding local financial data.

Connected delivery transactions are visible in the app and can be exported as CSV text from Settings. Because transaction rows and usage events are not retained by Unorder’s service, there are no server-side copies of those records to export or delete. You may contact us about an applicable account-data request.

Plaid and Apple may retain information under their own policies. Operational service-provider logs and backups, where used, are protected and expire under their configured retention schedules.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords, bank credentials, or payment-card numbers.

Children and changes

Unorder is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes and will post a new effective date before materially different practices take effect.
