Unorder support

Most setup issues come down to Screen Time permission, a selected app or website, or the current danger-window schedule.

Contact us

Email s85191939@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Never email a password, verification code, or full card number.

Protection is not appearing

  1. Open Unorder → Protect and confirm Protection is on.
  2. Choose at least one app, category, or website.
  3. Approve Screen Time access when iOS asks.
  4. Confirm the current time is inside your danger window or your guardrail has been reached.

Family Controls require an iPhone signed into iCloud; Simulator behavior is limited.

Notifications are not appearing

Open Unorder → You and enable Danger-window reminders. You can also review notification permission in iOS Settings.

Delete local data

Removing Unorder from your iPhone deletes its local settings. You can also turn off protection or change selected apps at any time from the Protect screen.
