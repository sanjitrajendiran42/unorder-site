Unorder support

Most setup issues come down to Screen Time permission, a selected app, or a bank connection that needs refreshing.

Contact us

Email support@unorder.app. Include your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Never email a bank password, Plaid credential, or full card number.

Protection is not appearing

  1. Open Unorder → Protect and confirm Protection is on.
  2. Choose at least one app, category, or website.
  3. Approve Screen Time access when iOS asks.
  4. Confirm the current time is inside your danger window or your guardrail has been reached.

Family Controls must be tested on a physical iPhone signed into iCloud; Simulator behavior is limited.

Bank data is stale

Open Unorder → You and tap Refresh transactions. Some institutions take time to post pending transactions. If the connection reports an error, disconnect it and connect again.

Cancel or restore Plus

Open Unorder → You → Unorder Plus. Use Manage subscription to cancel or change a plan, or Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Delete data

Open Unorder → You → Delete account and imported data. Disconnecting a single bank removes that institution’s imported data. Deleting the app alone does not cancel a subscription.
