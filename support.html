Unorder support

Most setup issues come down to Screen Time permission, a selected app or website, or the current danger-window schedule.

Contact us

Email s85191939@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Never email a password, verification code, bank credential, or full card number.

Protection is not appearing

  1. Open Unorder → Protect and confirm Protection is on.
  2. Choose at least one app, category, or website.
  3. Approve Screen Time access when iOS asks.
  4. Confirm the current time is inside your danger window or your connected-data guardrail has been reached.

Family Controls require an iPhone signed into iCloud; Simulator behavior is limited.

Connected spending needs attention

Open Unorder → Spending to retry a sync or reconnect an institution. Bank feeds can be delayed, and Unorder cannot see prices or carts inside delivery apps. You can disconnect an institution even if Plus is no longer active.

Notifications are not appearing

Open Unorder → You and enable Danger-window reminders. You can also review notification permission in iOS Settings.

Delete your data

Open Unorder → You to disconnect a financial institution or delete your account and imported data. Removing Unorder from your iPhone deletes local settings but does not by itself delete a connected account or cancel an App Store subscription.
